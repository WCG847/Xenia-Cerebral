# Xenia Cerebral — THE Definitive XBOX 360 Emulator

## Overview
Looking to emulate your classics? Relive that mid 2000s nostalgia?  Look no further, **Xenia Cerebral** got you covered. **The Cerebral Emulator** is progressing towards being the definitive XBOX 360 emulator on the market. Plus, you can enhance your games. 2X, 3X, 4X? **YES**, we have it all here! No need to stare at sad low-quality pixels anymore — unless, of course, you really want to.

## Value Proposition
- Native Windows Optimisation
- Hardware Acceleration
- Free and Open Source
- Easy to Build; Easy to Contribute
- DirectX support
- .slnx support; MSBuild compatibility


## Target Demographic
- Consumers: Broad range of customers. Anyone is free to try this out!
- Developers: People who know C, C++, the MSBuild build system, MSVC/Microsoft/Visual Studio expertise and MASM, specialising in INTEL64 optimisation. Windows-first developers are preferred though anyone can contribute! DirectX developers, low-level graphics programmers, or anyone coming from a graphical technical background, you are much welcome.


## Striving
We strive towards supporting many of your favourite classics on Windows, though it's going to be a tough hill. Please, raise issues, ask us at Discussions and comment on commits, your input means a lot.
